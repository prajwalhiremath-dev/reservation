About Our Cisco Reservation Dashboard

Welcome to our Testbed Reservation Dashboard, a platform where engineers can reserve Nexus L3 switches for testing and automation purposes. This system ensures smooth and robust reservations with no overlap, even handling concurrent requests.

Users receive Webex notifications for updates, including reminders 5 minutes before their reservation ends. Our platform also offers analytical and graphical insights, helping users and testbed managers track usage and performance effectively.

This project is developed by aprajwal as part of the N39K team.
